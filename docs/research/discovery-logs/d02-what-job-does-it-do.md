### 1. What I did
- I picked a product I use on a daily basis: the CRM GoHighLevel.
- What does GHL do for me: It allows me to see all leads generated from each
  client I work for.
- It allows me to contact those leads — by email, phone, sms, or whatsapp.
- It allows me to book appointments with those leads all while linking to my
  google calendar.
- The last time I used it was today to call a lead.
- Without it, I would not be able to perform my job independently, as I would
  have to rely on others to make my schedule.
