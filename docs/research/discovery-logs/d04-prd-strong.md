compared them side by side. This will assist me in writing out my own prd.

### 2. Artifact
The two PRDs I examined were from Airbnb and Spotify.

**Airbnb** —
[PRD for Airbnb Total Price Display](https://ginger-petunia-6a1.notion.site/Product-Requirements-Document-PRD-for-Airbnb-Total-Price-Display-b2191e85baf441ea8f565dad9649e463)
- Took 20 minutes to skim.
- First sentence — **Current State:** Airbnb currently displays only the
  nightly rate on search and listing pages. **Problem:** This leads to
  confusion and dissatisfaction due to unexpected additional costs.
  **Opportunity:** Enhancing transparency can improve user experience and
  comply with various global regulations.
- What was addressed: Having prices immediately visible for the user.
- My conclusions: Very short and straightforward PRD. Explains how third
  party APIs were needed to display prices immediately, and layed out time to
  launch. Formatting is very simple for someone not well versed in software
  engineering and easy to digest.

**Spotify** —
[Product Requirements Document](https://assets.nextleap.app/submissions/ProductRequirementsDocument-1-588a1cc9-37e7-440e-91d4-3d74f55ee1aa.pdf)
- Took 15 minutes to skim.
- First sentence: 18–24 year olds engage less than Spotify wants due to
  content saturation and no social interaction. Light listeners 18–34 are 33%
  of the user base; they disengage because of ad interruptions, difficulty
  discovering music, and a cluttered interface. Business value: engagement,
  retention, conversion. Urgency: industry CAGR of 15.4%.
- Much more measurable problem given the data. Engagement numbers are
  directly stated rather than comparing to vague "user experience."
- What was addressed: How to switch around revenue generators to encourage
  more listening time and eventually premium subscriptions.
- My conclusions: I liked this PRD much more than the Airbnb one, because the
  problem was backed up with evidence rather than an abstract user
  experience.

### 3. Claim
A PRD must have a problem backed up by numbers and evidence in order to
establish urgency and potential ROI.
