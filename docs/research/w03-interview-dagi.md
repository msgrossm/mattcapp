## What this actually is, and isn't, evidence for

- **Single interview subject.** Everything above is one person, one ticket,
  one country (Ethiopia — outside the concept brief's NY/USA scope). D03
  already flagged this; repeating it here so it isn't lost the next time
  someone reads only the stories and not the source.
- **Row 5 is new material.** It doesn't describe a fact about tickets — it
  describes *how Dagi decided*: fast, low-effort, no comparison of options.
  That's a real (if thin) basis for a 5th story about wanting a single clear
  recommendation rather than information to research further.
- **Row 3 and row 4** both hinge on "because it was in Ethiopia" — i.e. Dagi's
  own experience (no insurance impact, cop stated the cost verbally) may not
  generalize to a NY ticket at all. These rows support a *question* to ask a
  future interviewee more than they support a NY-specific requirement.
- The assignment expects 8–12 stories from Lab 3. This is the only interview
  transcript found on disk or in Drive (searched "interview", "backlog",
  "ticket" — nothing else surfaced). **The honest state of the evidence base
  is one interviewee**, not eight to twelve. That gap belongs in the PRD's
  open questions, not papered over.
